The journey inside

From understanding your struggle to shaping your future.

Student life can involve more than lessons, assignments and exams. There can also be expectations, comparison, fear of failure and thoughts that are difficult to explain to others.

This book is organised as a four-part journey: first recognising those struggles, then examining the inner battles, shifting your mindset and finally focusing on habits and transformation.

Explore the chapter list below to see the subjects covered before deciding whether the book is right for you.
